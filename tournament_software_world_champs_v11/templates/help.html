README / Help

Tournament Software - operating guide and tournament logic.
Back to Dashboard

1. Tournament systems

Group Stage + Championship runs round-robin groups first and then builds Championship and, when enabled, exact-placement/consolation paths. Single Elimination removes a team from the title path after one loss. Double Elimination uses a winners/losers concept. Round Robin is league play. Swiss pairs teams over multiple rounds without a full round-robin.

2. Seeding and groups

Players can be imported from Excel using columns Rank and Name. Seeding can also be changed by drag and drop. Group assignment can be automatic or manual. Within a group, the highest original seed remains the highest group seed.

3. Group standings and qualification

Group tables are calculated from completed group matches. The standard order is points, wins, set difference and point difference, with original seed used only as a final deterministic tie-break where needed. In Settings, Qualifiers per group controls the automatic places. Best teams from next place adds wildcards, for example 2 qualifiers per group + 2 best teams from 3rd place.

When groups have different sizes, wildcard comparison is normalized per match in the current engine so teams are not rewarded simply for playing more group matches.

4. Tournament Seed after groups

Qualified teams receive a post-group Tournament Seed. Group winners are ranked ahead of runners-up, then qualified teams from the next group position. Inside each band the engine compares group performance (points per match / win rate / set and point performance) and preserves original seeding as a final tie-break.

5. Pairing and rematch avoidance

The automatic pairing proposal protects the strongest Tournament Seeds and tries to pair them against lower-ranked opponents. In the first knockout round the engine avoids a rematch between teams that already met in the same group whenever a mathematically valid alternative exists. Manual pairing can override the proposal while the relevant round is still editable.

6. Reseeding modes

  • First knockout round only - the first post-group round can be seeded/manual; after it is confirmed, winners follow the fixed bracket tree.
  • Reseed every round - after a round is complete, the surviving teams can be reseeded and the next round can be manually adjusted.
  • Fixed bracket - all later paths follow the generated tree; no reseeding.

A round is editable until an actual user-entered result in that round makes the competitive path live. BYEs and automatic propagation alone do not count as a played result.

7. Protected BYEs

If the qualified field does not fill a complete 4/8/16/32 bracket, the application can allocate unavoidable BYEs to the highest Tournament Seeds. Example: with 10 qualified teams, Seeds #1-#6 receive BYEs into the 8-team core, while Seeds #7-#10 play two preliminary matches. This protects top seeds without inventing extra qualifiers.

8. Consolation / exact placement

With Exact placement / consolation enabled, losing a Championship match does not automatically end the tournament. Losers are routed into placement matches so final places are decided on court. Semi-final losers play for 3rd place. Future draw slots show labels such as Winner #21 or Loser #16, so the complete route is visible before the participant is known.

9. Match order and courts

Group matches are scheduled first. Multiple groups are interleaved across the available courts. Post-group matches are activated after group qualification is known. Match numbers are intended as persistent operational identifiers and downstream matches must always appear after the matches they depend on.

10. Scoring

Each phase can use Best of 3 or Best of 5 and race to 15 or 21. Scores are entered set by set. Win-by-two/deuce scores are accepted. Empty set rows are ignored.

11. Backup and exports

Backup JSON stores tournament settings, players, phases and match results. Results Excel exports final standings and matches. PDF exports provide Groups & Results, Match Schedule, Tournament Draws and Final Standings. Tournament logos can be uploaded and appear as a subtle application watermark and a small PDF mark.

12. Recommended workflow

  1. Create the tournament and set courts/system.
  2. Import or enter teams and confirm seeding.
  3. Set group count and automatic/manual assignment.
  4. Generate structure and play all Group Stage matches.
  5. Review final group tables and qualified teams.
  6. Confirm or manually adjust the allowed knockout pairing.
  7. Enter results round by round.
  8. Use Tournament Draws to verify Championship and consolation paths.
  9. Export PDFs/Excel and create a Backup JSON regularly.
